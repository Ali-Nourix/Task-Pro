## Layout
Centered 1100px desktop canvas, 224px labeled navigation rail. Main content uses 12 equal columns with 24px gaps: task workspace 8 columns and planning/context 4 columns. Arithmetic: 8 + 4 = 12; both region edges close on the same content frame. At 1050–1279 reduce gap to16px. Under1050 use a single reading column with a reserved bottom navigation region, never covering content. Mobile day strip stays compact; completing and opening tasks remain separate controls.
